## 📌 Executive Summary

Urban home-service technicians (electricians, plumbers, carpenters) are locked into extractive commercial platforms that charge *25%–35% commissions*, enforce opaque algorithmic shadow-banning, and provide zero ownership or social security.

*Servio* restructures gig work as an open, worker-owned digital platform cooperative under the Multi-State Cooperative Societies framework:
* *Workers are legal shareholders, taking home **90%* of every transaction.
* *Customer acquisition cost (CAC) is zero, tapping consumer apps via the open **ONDC network*.
* *Work distribution is democratic*, driven by an open-source, anti-monopoly dispatch engine.
